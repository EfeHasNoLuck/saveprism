# Disclaimer

This document explains what SavePrism is for, what it is not for, and where your responsibility starts.

## What SavePrism is

A local desktop editor for save files. It reads a save, shows its contents as a tree, lets you change values, and writes the file back. Nothing is uploaded and no telemetry is collected by default.

## What SavePrism is not

SavePrism does not edit online or multiplayer games, bypass anti-cheat or DRM, edit game memory, or act as a trainer. It does not ship game assets, encryption keys, or other people's saves.

## Your responsibility

- Edit only saves of single-player games that you own.
- Editing a save can corrupt it or break your game. It can also violate a game's terms of service, including account bans in games with online features. You accept that risk.
- SavePrism makes a backup before every write, but it gives no guarantee. The software is provided "AS IS" under the MIT license, without warranty of any kind.

## Keys

SavePrism does not distribute encryption keys. Keys it uses are derived on your machine from game files you own.
